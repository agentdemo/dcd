Sushill Waddhwa | International Entrepreneur & Investor

Sushill Waddhwa is an international entrepreneur, investor, and Founder & CMD of Platinum World Grroup. He divides his time between Mumbai, Dubai, Sri Lanka and Europe.

His work has taken him across 82 countries and 2,040+ luxury hotels, building relationships with hotels, destinations, planners and hospitality leaders worldwide. His entrepreneurial interests span events and wedding tourism, hospitality, investments, lifestyle experiences, media and entertainment.

Business

  • Platinum World Grroup: a global B2B company serving event, wedding, celebration and travel planners through luxury hotel buyouts, group bookings and destination services.
  • Artoso Hospitality Corp: an AI-powered lifestyle company operating boutique hotels and resorts in key destinations worldwide.
  • Platinum World Pictures: a media, film and entertainment venture producing premium content for luxury brands, hotels and tourism boards.
  • Platinum World Lifestyle: a discreet lifestyle advisory service for private aviation, superyachts, luxury real estate and residency.
  • Family Office Investments: an active portfolio of real estate and investments across hospitality, energy, technology and AI.
